CSX texture streaming was developed with reference to SPS. The reference
covers distance-based demand, GPU copies of lower mip levels, a restoration
RAM buffer, adjustable VRAM reserve, remembered load sizes and a size cap.

SPS's src/TextureStream.cpp also documents the DDS-loader ABI and its
SE/AE/VR locations (75721, 77533, 0xDD2B60), and the texture-creation slot
of BSShaderResourceManager (0xD0 / 8 = 0x1A). These integration details are
acknowledged at the corresponding CSX hook sites.

CSX has its own streaming policy, stereo demand calculation, validated DDS
reader, bounded restoration cache, remembered-size format, upload and GPU
retirement code. SPS implementation files, binaries and assets are not
bundled.
